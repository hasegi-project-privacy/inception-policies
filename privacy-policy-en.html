Inception Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-20 · Effective: 2026-05-20

Hasegi Inc. ("we") values your privacy and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and relevant laws.

1. Information We Collect

The Service does not require sign-up, so we do not collect personally identifiable information such as name, email, or phone number. The following data is processed automatically:

TypeDetailsStorage
Learning stateBookmarks, recently viewed sentences, streak countsLocal device (SharedPreferences)
Advertising identifieriOS IDFA / Android Ad ID (with user consent)Google AdMob servers
Device & usageOS, app version, device model (for ad delivery)Google AdMob servers

2. Purposes

  • Preserve learning progress (locally on device)
  • Deliver personalized advertising (only when tracking is permitted)
  • Improve service quality and statistical analysis

3. Advertising SDK

The Service uses Google AdMob. AdMob delivers ads using non-personally-identifiable advertising IDs. You can opt out at any time:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking → toggle off "Inception"
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Reset advertising ID" / "Opt out of personalized ads"

Google's advertising policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. In-App Purchase Information

The Service offers a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase ("Inception Premium"). All payments are processed entirely through Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not directly collect or retain any payment information such as card numbers or bank details. The only purchase-related data we recognize is the ownership flag, stored locally on your device.

5. Retention

We do not retain personally identifiable information on our servers. Learning data stored locally is deleted when the app is removed.

6. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share your information with third parties except as required by law or as described above for advertising delivery to Google AdMob.

7. Processors

We may entrust Google LLC (AdMob) with the processing of advertising identifiers for ad delivery purposes.

8. User Rights

You may opt out of advertising identifier use at any time. To delete your learning data, simply uninstall the app. For additional rights, contact us below.

9. Children Under 14

The Service is available to children under 14, but since we do not collect identifiable personal information, no separate guardian consent process is implemented.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Policy in response to legal or service changes; revisions take effect upon posting on this page.
